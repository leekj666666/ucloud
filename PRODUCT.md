## Product Principles

- Make evidence, proposed change and actual business outcome visible together.
- Keep decisions attributable and tied to immutable plan identity.
- Distinguish pending, failed, cancelled, escalated and verified recovery.
- Use real data and preserve uncertainty and failures.
