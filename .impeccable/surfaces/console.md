# 故障指挥台

Mode: Operate.

Audience: competition judges watching a live workflow, plus the operator conducting it.

First viewport: event selection on the left; the actual incident, service and five-stage workflow in the center; the precise plan and human approval on the right. The reader can identify the problem, current evidence, next decision and recovery claim without scrolling through a marketing introduction.

Visitor path: select/create → investigate → inspect cited evidence → review exact diff → approve/reject → observe the independent business window → export real records.

Signature interaction: a cited evidence chip opens the corresponding live record, while the approval pane retains the exact target and version being reviewed. Stage selection reveals that stage's evidence or verification; it never invents progress.

Quiet states: no event selected, no console tasks yet, waiting for telemetry, waiting for an operator, interrupted transport and completed failure. Every state names the next available action.

Verification: one batched desktop/mobile browser review, one correction batch and at most one confirmation capture round for visual polish. Functional/security tests are separate acceptance requirements.
